Privacy Policy

Your trust matters to us. Read how we protect your information.

Last Updated: April 16, 2026

1. Introduction

Wealth Master Academy ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, fill out forms, enroll in courses, or interact with our Meta (Facebook/Instagram) ads. It complies with applicable Indian data protection laws and Meta's advertising policies.

📢 For Meta Ads (Facebook/Instagram): We use conversion tracking pixels and custom audiences. Any data collected via Meta ads is used solely for advertising performance analysis and retargeting, in full compliance with Meta's terms.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Identification Information: Name, email address, phone number, city, course interest (provided via enquiry forms).
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, time spent – collected via cookies and similar technologies.
  • Advertising Data: Meta Pixel data (e.g., clicks on ads, conversions) to measure campaign effectiveness and build custom audiences.
  • Payment Information: If you enroll in paid courses, we collect billing details through secure third-party gateways. We do not store full payment card data.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your data for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your enquiries and provide course information.
  • To process enrollments and deliver course content (online/offline).
  • To improve our website, courses, and user experience.
  • To run Meta (Facebook/Instagram) advertising campaigns, including retargeting and lookalike audiences.
  • To send important updates, promotional offers (with your consent), and policy changes.
  • To comply with legal obligations and prevent fraud.

4. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies, web beacons, and Meta Pixel to enhance your browsing experience and analyze ad performance. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Essential cookies cannot be disabled as they are necessary for website functionality.

By using our website, you consent to our use of cookies as described in this policy. For Meta ads, the Meta Pixel collects data about your interactions with our ads and website – this helps us show relevant offers and measure conversions.

5. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers: Trusted third parties (e.g., payment gateways, email platforms, Meta) that help us operate our business – they are contractually bound to protect your data.
  • Legal Compliance: If required by law, court order, or government regulation.
  • Business Transfers: In case of merger, acquisition, or asset sale, your data may be transferred.

We ensure all partners comply with applicable data protection standards.

6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures (SSL encryption, access controls, regular audits) to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure – you share data at your own risk.

7. Your Rights (For Indian Users)

Under Indian law (including upcoming Digital Personal Data Protection Act), you have the right to:

  • Access and obtain a copy of your personal data.
  • Rectify inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communications.
  • Request deletion of your data, subject to legal retention requirements.
  • Lodge a complaint with the relevant data protection authority.

To exercise these rights, contact us at wealthmaster912@gmail.com.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If we become aware of such data, we will delete it promptly.

9. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external sites (e.g., payment partners, social media). We are not responsible for their privacy practices – please review their policies independently.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last Updated" date at the top indicates when changes were made. Continued use of our website after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
